Generate two images, one at a time, and copy each to its path before starting the next.

1. ./raw/build-tall.png
First load ./raw/build.png with view_image: it is the reference for the parts, materials, surface, light and camera. Make the same overhead flat-lay (camera looking straight down, same plaster surface, same soft daylight from upper left with short soft shadows to the lower right, same grain), recomposed for a tall phone screen. Portrait 2:3.
The same parts and no others: four honed travertine blocks of stepped heights, two long brass rails, four short brass posts, three matte-black steel hoops of three sizes. No ball.
Layout, top to bottom, everything parallel and tidy with even gaps:
- The four blocks stand side by side in one row, tallest on the left stepping down to the shortest on the right, their bottom edges aligned on one line. The row spans from about 10 percent to 90 percent of the frame width. The top of the tallest block is about 22 percent down the frame and the row's bottom edge about 46 percent down.
- Directly beneath, the two brass rails lie horizontally, one above the other, spanning the same width as the block row.
- Beneath the rails, one row: the four posts standing upright side by side on the left half, the three hoops side by side on the right half, smallest to largest. This row ends about 68 percent down the frame.
The top fifth of the frame and the bottom 30 percent are completely empty plaster.
